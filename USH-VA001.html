"Cornice Crux" arrives as the first various-artists release on USHBA Records, USH-VA001 — four artists gathered at the edge where the ridge gives way. True to the label's guiding principle, the collection binds the raw, untamed splendour of the natural world to the meticulous craft of techno. AMØK, Elias Garcia, Fukumachi, and Translate each offer a distinct descent, threading driving rhythms through evocative melody, sound born of shadow and stone that resounds far beyond the closing note.
